Panope — Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 4, 2026

Panope is a personal dive logbook app. It has no company, no user accounts, and no server of its own — it's built and maintained by an individual developer. This page explains what the app does with your information.

What the app stores

Everything you log lives in two places, both under your own control:

  • On your device, in the app's own local database.
  • In your own private iCloud account, so your logbook can sync between your own devices. This uses Apple's CloudKit — the developer has no access to this data; it sits in a private database tied to your Apple ID.

The developer does not operate a server, does not receive a copy of your dive log, and cannot see your data.

What the app collects

DataWhy
Dive details you enter — site, times, depth, water temperature, notes, buddy/dive centre name The core purpose of the app: your logbook
Location — coordinates you place for a dive site (never inferred automatically from a site name) Shown on the map, and used to look up sea/air conditions and a site description for that location
Apple Health data — underwater diving workouts, depth, water temperature, weather metadata So your dives appear in Apple Health and Fitness, and so the app can read existing dives already in Health (e.g. from a dive computer or Apple Watch)

Health access is granted or denied through Apple's own permission system, not by Panope — you can review or revoke it at any time in the Health app, under Sharing → Apps.

Third-party services this app talks to

These requests carry only a coordinate and/or a site name — never your name, account, or any other identifying information:

  • Open-Meteo — sea-surface temperature and wave height for a dive's coordinates and date.
  • Apple WeatherKit — historical air temperature and sky conditions for a dive's coordinates and date.
  • Wikipedia / Wikimedia — a short article about a dive site, when one exists near its coordinates.
  • OpenStreetMap (Overpass API) — looking up dive sites not already in the app's bundled offline catalogue.
  • SSI (my.divessi.com) — if you choose to import your SSI logbook, you sign in directly on SSI's own website inside the app. Panope never sees your SSI password; it only reads the CSV file you export.

What this app does not do

  • No advertising, no ad networks, no advertising identifiers
  • No analytics or usage tracking
  • No user accounts, sign-up, or login of its own
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties

Your choices

  • Delete any dive, or all of them, from within the app at any time — Settings has a "Delete all dives" option that also removes the matching Apple Health workouts where possible.
  • Revoke Health access any time in the Health app under Sharing → Apps.
  • Deleting the app removes its local data; deleting it and its iCloud data (Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage) removes the synced copy too.

Children's privacy

Panope isn't directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect information from anyone under 13.
